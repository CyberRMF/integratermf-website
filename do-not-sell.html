Privacy Rights

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

✓ The short answer: CyberRMF LLC does not sell your personal information for money, and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. There is nothing to opt out of — that is our default and only setting.

What this page is for

California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and similar laws in Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, and other US states, give consumers the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information, and to opt out of "targeted advertising." This page exists to make that opt-out available even though our answer to the underlying question is we don't do those things.

What "sale" and "share" mean, and what we actually do

  • "Sale" under CCPA means disclosing personal information to a third party for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not do this.
  • "Sharing" under CPRA means disclosing personal information to a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising, whether or not for money. We do not do this.
  • "Targeted advertising" under the Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and similar laws means using personal information from your interactions with unaffiliated websites/apps to serve you ads based on inferred interests. We do not do this.
  • We do share personal information with a small set of vetted service providers / processors strictly to operate the site and service — hosting (Vercel), license management (Keygen), transactional email (Resend), source and download hosting (GitHub). Under CCPA these providers are "service providers" (not third parties), and under GDPR they are "processors." See our Privacy Statement § 4 for the full list.

Submit an opt-out request anyway

If you would like a formal record of your opt-out, or you're a compliance team documenting due-diligence for your own program, submit a request one of the following ways:

By email

Send an email to privacy@integratermf.com with subject line Do Not Sell or Share Request. Include your name, the work email you used when signing up, and (for eligibility) your US state of residence.

Open pre-filled email →

Via Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor Global Privacy Control browser signals as opt-out requests for California residents. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we will treat that as an opt-out for the browser session, and no further action from you is required.

What happens after you submit

  1. We will confirm receipt within a reasonable timeframe.
  2. We will verify your identity by matching the information you provide against our records. Because most CyberRMF signups use a business email, we may ask you to reply from that email address to confirm ownership.
  3. We will respond within 45 days of the request (extendable by an additional 45 days where permitted, with notice to you).
  4. Because CyberRMF does not sell or share personal information, we will confirm that no changes to our processing are required to honor your opt-out.

Related rights

For requests to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to appeal a denied request, see Section 8 of our Privacy Statement and email privacy@integratermf.com.

Authorized agents (California)

You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. We will require written proof of authorization (for example, a signed letter of authorization or a power of attorney) and, where appropriate, direct verification with you before acting on the agent's request.
