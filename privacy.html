Privacy Statement

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Statement explains how CyberRMF LLC ("CyberRMF," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, protects, and shares personal information when you visit integratermf.com or request access to our beta or trial programs. We publish this notice to satisfy applicable transparency obligations under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and comparable US state privacy laws.

1. Who we are

CyberRMF LLC is a US-based company that develops CyberRMF Integrate and CyberRMF Admin Tools — desktop applications for cyber compliance workflow, Risk Management Framework (RMF) artifact management, inventory, STIG validation, and related functions. For purposes of the GDPR, CyberRMF is the data controller for the personal information described in this Statement.

Contact: info@cyberrmf.com

2. What we collect

When you submit the beta or trial request form, we collect the following information you provide:

  • First and last name
  • Work email address
  • Organization name
  • Industry (from a drop-down, or free-text if you select "Other")
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Whether you have consented to our Terms and Conditions and to this Privacy Statement, and (if provided) your consent to receive optional marketing communications
  • Date and time of your submission

Automatically when you visit the site or use the beta/trial application server-side endpoints, we may collect:

  • IP address, user agent string, referring URL, and general geographic region (city/country level)
  • Strictly-necessary technical cookies and local storage entries (for example, your selected color theme)
  • Timestamps and status codes for form submissions and license activation/deactivation events

Software telemetry: The CyberRMF Integrate and Admin Tools desktop applications themselves do not collect telemetry, usage analytics, or crash reports, and do not transmit data to CyberRMF servers during normal operation. See our Terms and Conditions § 11 for the full statement on in-application data handling.

The site and services are intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with a small number of vetted service providers ("processors" / "service providers") who process it on our behalf, under their own privacy and security programs, and only to the extent necessary to operate the site and the beta/trial program. Our current subprocessors are:

  • Vercel Inc. — United States
  • Keygen — United States
  • Resend Inc. — United States

The subprocessor most directly relevant to how your personal information is used is Resend. When we send you a licensing or beta-related email, your name and email address are handed to Resend's servers solely so Resend can deliver that email to your inbox. We do not authorize Resend to use your information for any other purpose, and Resend does not retain the contents of our emails beyond what is necessary to deliver them. We do not use third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing-analytics companies.

A current subprocessor list is available on request at info@cyberrmf.com.

We may disclose personal information without your further consent when required by law, subpoena, court order, or lawful request from a governmental, regulatory, or law enforcement authority with competent jurisdiction, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, the safety of individuals, or the integrity of our systems. Where permitted by law, we will make reasonable efforts to notify affected individuals before responding.

5. How we protect it

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit and encryption at rest with our hosting and email providers.
  • Access to production data limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Environment isolation between development and production, with secrets managed as environment variables and never committed to source control.
  • Logging and monitoring of administrative access to production systems.
  • Written data processing agreements with our subprocessors.
  • A documented incident-response and breach-notification process.

No system is perfectly secure. If we experience a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected individuals and applicable regulatory authorities within the timeframes required by law.

6. International data transfers

Our infrastructure and personnel are located in the United States. If you access the site or submit information from outside the U.S. — including from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland — your personal information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Where required, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021 modules) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum with our processors as a lawful transfer mechanism.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Port a copy of your information to another provider in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Opt out of "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising — CyberRMF does none of these, but you may still submit a request through our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.
  • Not be subject to solely automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you — we do not make such decisions with your data.
  • Appeal a denial of a privacy rights request (residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and certain other states).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@cyberrmf.com with the phrase "Privacy request" in the subject line. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law — generally 30 days under GDPR, and 45 days under CCPA (extendable by an additional 45 days where permitted and with notice to you). We may need to verify your identity by asking you to confirm information already on file before we act on your request.

Authorized agents (California): California residents may designate an authorized agent to submit requests on their behalf. We will require proof of authorization and, where appropriate, direct verification with the consumer.

"Shine the Light" (California Civil Code § 1798.83): California residents may request information about disclosures of personal information to third parties for their direct-marketing purposes. We do not make such disclosures.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only a minimal set of technologies necessary to operate the site, including:

  • Strictly-necessary local storage to remember your selected color theme across visits.
  • Server-side logs generated by our hosting provider (Vercel) that record standard request information such as IP address, user agent, and timestamps.

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If we add non-essential analytics or measurement technologies in the future, we will request your consent through a banner or preference center where required by applicable law and will update this Statement accordingly.

10. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Our site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals because there is no consistent industry standard. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as opt-out-of-sale/share requests where applicable, though we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in any case.

11. Children

The site and the CyberRMF applications are directed to businesses and are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact info@cyberrmf.com and we will delete it.

Personal information submitted through our beta or trial forms is treated as commercial contact information (not Controlled Unclassified Information / CUI) and is not designed to hold data subject to DFARS 252.204-7012 or CMMC controls. If you have questions about our security posture for federal customers, please see our Security & Trust page or contact info@cyberrmf.com.
