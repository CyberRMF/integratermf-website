Security & Trust

Last updated: September 29, 2026

CyberRMF is built by cyber-compliance practitioners for cyber-compliance practitioners. This page summarizes how we protect our platform, our customers' data, and the desktop applications we distribute. For a dedicated privacy notice, see our Privacy Statement. To report a security concern, see the Report a Vulnerability section below.

1. Application security posture

CyberRMF Integrate and CyberRMF Admin Tools are desktop applications that run on the Licensee's own machines. This design deliberately keeps operational and inventory data inside the customer environment:

No telemetry

The applications collect no usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind.

No phone home

No connections to CyberRMF servers during normal operation. Third-party integrations are strictly opt-in and outbound only.

Local data only

All artifacts, scan results, and inventory data are stored in the Licensee's designated Workspace directory.

Credential handling

Domain administrator credentials used by Admin Tools remain in memory only and are cleared after 10 minutes of inactivity. Never persisted, never transmitted.

Integration credentials at rest

API tokens for SIEM, EDR, and vulnerability management platforms are encrypted using Electron's safeStorage (OS-native secure storage) when available.

Read-only by default

Admin Tools performs only enumeration and read operations against inventory-listed hosts. It does not perform subnet sweeps or discovery beyond the assets you explicitly list.

The full behavioral specification — including handling of TLS certificate verification, SSH host keys, and WinRM configuration changes — is documented in our Terms and Conditions, Sections 5–8.

3. Compliance posture

What CyberRMF is

CyberRMF is a tool that helps you demonstrate compliance with NIST SP 800-53, DISA STIGs, and related U.S. Government cyber frameworks. Its output (artifacts, POA&Ms, SCTM, CONMON, STIG results) is designed to feed your ATO package or organizational compliance program.

What CyberRMF is not

The current product is a locally-installed desktop application. It does not process Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on our infrastructure and does not itself require a FedRAMP authorization to operate. The beta signup form and license-issuance endpoints described in this page handle commercial contact information only.

Alignment

Our internal engineering and business practices are aligned with the following frameworks. Formal attestations are on our roadmap:

  • NIST SP 800-171 — internal controls for handling any customer-provided CUI we may receive under specific engagement terms.
  • CMMC 2.0 Level 2 — alignment for future DoD subcontracting where applicable.
  • SOC 2 Type I / Type II — on our roadmap once the beta enters general availability.
  • GDPR / CCPA / peer US state privacy laws — see the Privacy Statement.

4. Incident response and breach notification

We maintain a documented incident-response process covering triage, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review. If we experience a personal-data breach that is likely to result in risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we will notify affected individuals and applicable regulators within the timeframes required by law (generally 72 hours to the supervisory authority under GDPR, and "without unreasonable delay" under CCPA and state breach-notification laws).

5. Report a Vulnerability

We welcome coordinated disclosure from security researchers, customers, and the public. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in integratermf.com, the CyberRMF Integrate application, or CyberRMF Admin Tools:

  1. Email security@integratermf.com with a description of the issue, the affected component and version, and steps to reproduce.
  2. Please do not perform any testing that would degrade service for other customers, exfiltrate customer data, or access data beyond the minimum necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.
  3. We will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days, provide a substantive response within 15 business days, and coordinate with you on a disclosure timeline.
  4. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will credit reporters (with your permission) when a fix ships.

Our machine-readable security contact is published at /.well-known/security.txt per RFC 9116.

6. Data-processing agreements and enterprise inquiries

Customers who need a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA), a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), a completed vendor security questionnaire (CAIQ, SIG, or custom), a subprocessor list, an Insurance Certificate, or a copy of our W-9, please email info@cyberrmf.com with your request and organization name.

For DoD and federal buyers: we can provide additional materials on our NIST 800-171 self-assessment status, subprocessor location, and CMMC alignment on request. Please contact us early in your evaluation cycle so we can respond within your acquisition timeline.
